# DIVYA.EXE ✦ ElevenLabs & D-ID Speech Studio 👽💚

A cool, playful **Y2K cyber-kawaii** voice narration studio built with **HTML5, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript**, starring **Divya** — your green alien narrator, powered by the **ElevenLabs API** and integrated with the **D-ID Interactive Agent**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Starring Divya (Centerpiece Narrator)**:
  - Framed in a retro sticker-covered CRT monitor stage.
  - Interactive speech bubble giving live feedback and dialogue.
  - Reactive soundwaves, pulsating green aura, and head-bob animation when Divya speaks.
  - Clickable easter eggs: Click Divya for playful alien voice lines!

- **D-ID AI Live Agent Integration**:
  - Live D-ID conversational agent integrated directly on the page (`did-agent`, client key configured).

- **ElevenLabs Model Selection**:
  - **`Eleven Multilingual v2`**: Top expressiveness in 29+ languages.
  - **`Eleven Turbo v2.5`**: Low latency (~250ms), ideal for fast pacing.
  - **`Eleven Flash v2.5`**: Ultra-fast (~75ms) and cost-effective.
  - **`Eleven English v1`**: Classic clear narrative English.
  - **`Eleven Multilingual v1`**: Original multi-language synthesis.

- **Speaker Voice Picker & Account Sync**:
  - Curated default voices with Divya-recommended presets (*Rachel, Bella, Domi, Elli, Adam, Antoni, Charlie, etc.*).
  - One-click **SYNC** button to fetch custom voice clones from your personal ElevenLabs account.

- **Advanced Tone & Acoustics Tuning**:
  - **Stability**: Dial between emotional variability and steady narration.
  - **Clarity / Similarity**: Fine-tune voice likeness.
  - **Style Exaggeration**: Amplify voice personality.
  - **Speaker Boost**: Enhance natural timbre.

- **Audio Visualizer & Retro Player**:
  - Real-time HTML5 `<canvas>` frequency visualizer with neon lime and cyan audio waves.
  - Timeline seeking slider with current and total duration.
  - Play, pause, 5s rewind, 5s forward, stop controls.
  - Playback speed switcher (`0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2.0x`).
  - Volume slider with one-click mute/unmute.
  - Direct `.mp3` download button.

- **Script Editor**:
  - Character counter with estimated speech duration.
  - Quick-start sticker sample templates (*Story*, *Anime Intro*, *Trailer*, *Tech News*).

- **Generation Log (History)**:
  - Saves your recent narrations in `localStorage` with timestamps and one-click script restore.
